Tips for Writing Your Motivation Letter

Be Personal & Genuine

Write about what genuinely interests you about the course topic and why it matters to you. Avoid generic statements — let your personality shine through!

Show Your Motivation

Explain what you hope to learn and how the course aligns with your interests or career goals. Demonstrate enthusiasm for the opportunity.

Keep It Concise

Aim for 1–2 well-written paragraphs, not pages. Make every sentence count and avoid unnecessary words.

Highlight Your Openness

BEST Courses aren't just about learning — they're about making international connections. Show that you're excited to meet people from across Europe.

Proofread Carefully

Check spelling, grammar, and formatting. A well-written letter shows respect for the organizing group and attention to detail.

Be Authentic

Don't try to be someone you're not. Organizing groups want to meet the real you — your interests, perspectives, and personality matter!
