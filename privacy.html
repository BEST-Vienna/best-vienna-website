Privacy Policy

How we handle personal data and cookies on this website.

Last updated: 5 September 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by BEST Vienna (Board of European Students of Technology — Vienna Local Group), a student organisation based at TU Wien. Full details of our legal entity, registration and representatives are published in our Impressum.

For any question about this Privacy Policy or about your personal data, you can contact us at vienna@best-eu.org.

2. What data we process

Simply browsing this website does not require you to actively provide any personal data. Like most websites, our server automatically records basic technical information for each request (e.g. IP address, browser type, date and time) for a short period, to keep the site secure and operating correctly. If you contact us by email or through a linked form (e.g. our membership application), we process the information you voluntarily submit in order to reply to you or process your request.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

We only use two categories of storage on this website:

3.1 Strictly necessary

We store your cookie consent choice (accepted or rejected) in your browser's local storage so we don't ask you again on every visit. This does not require consent, as it is strictly necessary to operate the consent banner itself, and is never sent to a third party.

3.2 Google Analytics (only with your consent)

With your explicit, opt-in consent, we use Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; data may be processed by Google LLC in the United States) to understand how visitors use our website — e.g. which pages are popular — so we can improve it. Google Analytics is not loaded and sets no cookies until you click "Accept" in the cookie banner. If you click "Reject", or simply close the banner without accepting, Google Analytics is never loaded.

You can withdraw your consent at any time via the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of every page. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Cookies that may be set once you accept:

Cookie Purpose Retention
_ga Distinguishes unique users 13 months
_ga_<container-id> Persists session state for Google Analytics 4 13 months

Because Google may process this data in the United States, this transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses as implemented by Google. You can find more information in Google's Privacy Policy and opt out of Google Analytics across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Legal basis

We only load Google Analytics after you give your explicit, opt-in consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and § 165 of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021 — TKG 2021 — regarding storage of and access to information on your device). You are free to refuse or withdraw consent at any time, without any negative consequence for using this website.

5. Embedded third-party content

Some pages embed a Google Maps map to show our office location. Loading that embed may cause your browser to connect to Google's servers, which can process technical data such as your IP address, independently of your analytics consent choice, since it is needed to display the map. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Request correction of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Request erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Request restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Request data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Withdraw consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at vienna@best-eu.org. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Austrian data protection authority:

Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde (DSB)
Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria
dsb@dsb.gv.at · www.dsb.gv.at

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we change the tools we use on this website. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
