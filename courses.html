About BEST Courses

What Are BEST Courses?

Each summer, BEST Vienna students can apply for fully-funded 10-day academic courses hosted by one of 84 BEST groups across Europe. It's an incredible opportunity to dive deep into cutting-edge topics while exploring a new country and making friends from across the continent.

Whether you're interested in emerging technologies, business innovation, engineering challenges, or cultural exploration — there's a course for you. All costs are covered: travel, accommodation, meals, and lectures — so you just need to bring your enthusiasm and curiosity!

Course Highlights

  • ✓ Fully funded — no costs, travel & accommodation included
  • ✓ Hands-on workshops & lectures led by industry professionals
  • ✓ Network with students from across Europe
  • ✓ Explore a new country with fellow BEST Vienna members
APPLICATION PROCESS

How to Apply for a Course?

The application process is straightforward and designed to help the organizing group get to know you better.

1

Find Available Courses

Check which BEST groups are hosting courses this year and what topics they're covering. Each course offers unique content and experiences.

2

Prepare Your Application

Write a brief motivation letter explaining why you're interested in the course and what you hope to gain from the experience. See examples below to get inspired!

3

Submit Your Application

Send your motivation letter and any other required documents to the organizing group's email by the application deadline.

4

Get Selected

Wait for the organizing group to review applications and notify you of the results. The process usually takes a few weeks.

5

Prepare & Attend

Once selected, you'll receive all the details about logistics, travel, accommodation, and what to bring. Then — pack your bags and get ready for an amazing experience!

Key Information

🎓

Who Can Apply?

All TU Wien students (BSc, MSc, any field). You don't need any special background or experience — just curiosity and enthusiasm!

💰

Cost

Completely free for BEST Vienna members and TU Wien students! Travel, accommodation, meals, and courses are fully funded.

📅

When

Courses typically run in July and August. Each course is 10 days long. Deadlines vary by course, usually in April–May.

🗺️

Where

Across Europe! Courses are hosted by BEST groups in different cities — you get to choose which country interests you most.

🌍

Language

All courses are conducted in English. No additional language skills required — students from all over Europe attend.

✉️

Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a member of BEST Vienna to apply?

No! All TU Wien students can apply for BEST Courses, whether they're members of BEST Vienna or not. However, being part of the BEST Vienna community definitely makes the experience even better!

Can I choose which course to apply for?

Yes! Each summer, different BEST groups host courses on various topics and in different locations. You can choose which course interests you most and apply to that specific one.

What if I don't get selected?

Courses are popular and selective, so not everyone gets in on their first try. Don't be discouraged — you can apply again next year! Each year brings new courses, topics, and opportunities.

What's the time commitment?

Courses are 10 days long, typically in July or August. You'll spend most of the day in workshops and lectures, with evenings and free time for socializing and exploring. It's intensive but incredibly rewarding!

Will I get ECTS credits or a certificate?

This depends on the specific course and the organizing group. Many courses provide certificates of attendance, and some may offer ECTS credits. Check with the organizing group for details about your specific course.

Can I attend with friends from BEST Vienna?

Yes! Many BEST Vienna members attend the same course together. It's a great way to deepen friendships and have an amazing shared experience. Just remember that each person needs to submit their own application.
