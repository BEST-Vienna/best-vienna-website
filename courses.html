About BEST Courses

What Are BEST Courses?

TU Wien students can apply for roughly 10-day-long academic courses hosted by one of the 79 BEST groups across Europe.

Whether you're interested in emerging technologies, business innovation, engineering challenges, or cultural exploration, there's a course for you. All courses are made to be affordable for everyone and cost below 100€ (accommodation and food included), so you just need to bring your enthusiasm, curiosity and plane ticket!

When are BEST Courses happening?

The whole year! We differentiate between 4 seasons: Winter, Spring, Summer, Autumn and each season there will be different courses to choose from. Summer has the biggest selection most of the time.

APPLICATION PROCESS

How to Apply for a Course?

All BEST Courses are managed centrally on the official BEST website. Here's exactly what to do, step by step.

1

Create Your Account

Sign up on best.eu.org with your e-mail address. When asked for a "Student Identifier", just enter your TU Wien student ID (Matrikelnummer).

2

Browse Available Courses

Check the List of BEST Events for the current season. Applications open a few months before each season and stay open for about 30 days, so check back regularly.

3

Write Your Motivation Letter

Your cover letter is the main thing organizers judge your application on. Follow the official writing guide and check our sample letters for inspiration.

4

Submit & Track Your Application

You can apply to up to 3 courses per season (but be accepted to only 1). Applications can be edited anytime before the deadline — afterwards, track your status under My Applications.

5

Get Selected & Confirm

After the deadline, the organizing group reviews every application and validates accounts. If you're accepted (or placed on the waiting list), the Course Coordinator will reach out to confirm your spot and arrange the deposit.

Key Information

Who Can Apply?

All TU Wien students (BSc, MSc, any field). You don't need any special background or experience — just curiosity and enthusiasm!

Cost

Below 100€: Accommodation, meals, and course included. You only need to buy your transportation separately.

When

Courses are year round. Each course is roughly 10 days long. Deadlines vary by course.

Where

Across Europe! Courses are hosted by BEST groups in different cities, you get to choose which where you go.

Language

All courses are conducted in English. No additional language skills required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a member of BEST Vienna to apply?

No! All TU Wien students can apply for BEST Courses, whether they're members of BEST Vienna or not. However, being part of the BEST Vienna community definitely makes the experience even better!

Can I choose which course to apply for?

Yes! Different BEST groups host courses on various topics and in different locations throughout the year. You can apply to up to 3 courses per season which interest you most.

What if I don't get selected?

Courses are popular and selective, so not everyone gets in on their first try. Don't be discouraged, you can apply again next season! Each season brings new courses, topics, and opportunities.

What's the time commitment?

Courses are 10 days long. You'll spend most of the day in workshops and lectures, with evenings and free time for socializing and exploring. It's intensive but incredibly rewarding!

Will I get ECTS credits or a certificate?

This depends on the specific course and the organizing group. Many courses provide certificates of attendance that are recognized by TU for 1-1.5 ECTS. Check with the organizing group for details about your specific course.

Can I attend with friends from BEST Vienna?

Yes! Many BEST Vienna members attend the same course together. It's a great way to deepen friendships and have an amazing shared experience. Just remember that each person needs to submit their own application and there is a chance only one of you is accepted.
